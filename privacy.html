Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Project Y is a private, non-commercial personal project operated and used by one individual. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Information accessed

With the operator's explicit authorization, Project Y may access categories of the operator's Oura data needed for personal wellbeing tracking. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, stress, recovery, workout, session, and related account data.

Purpose of processing

The information is used only to help the operator review personal patterns in sleep, recovery, stress, activity, and overall wellbeing. It is not used for advertising, sale, profiling of other people, or training an artificial intelligence or machine-learning model.

Storage and sharing

Project Y is intended to process and store retrieved data locally on the operator's own device. Data is not sold, rented, published, or shared with other users or third parties, except where technically necessary to use Oura's authorized API or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls the locally stored information and may delete it at any time. Access to Oura data can be revoked through the relevant Oura account settings. When access is revoked or the project is discontinued, locally stored Oura data should be deleted when it is no longer needed for the stated personal purpose.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect access credentials and locally stored data. API credentials and access tokens are not intentionally published or shared.

Health information

Project Y supports personal reflection only. It is not a medical device, healthcare service, or diagnostic tool, and its output should not replace professional medical advice.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the project's data practices materially change. The current revision date appears at the top of this page.
